Privacy policy

Effective November 28, 2026

Interval Lab is a free music theory lab. You don't sign up, log in or type in any personal information to use it. This page explains the little that does happen with your information, in plain words.

What Interval Lab collects

Nothing that identifies you. Interval Lab has no accounts, no forms, no microphone or camera access, and no analytics or tracking of its own. All the sounds are made right in your browser, and nothing you play or answer is sent anywhere.

What's saved on your device

Interval Lab remembers two settings in your browser's local storage so the app opens the way you left it:

  • which tab you were on (Tuning, Harmonics, Intervals or Temperament)
  • whether you chose Simple or Advanced mode

These stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase them any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Ads

Interval Lab works fully offline, and it never shows ads while you're offline. When your device is online, the page may show an ad from Google AdSense at the bottom of the page. Ads never cover the lessons or the sound.

When an ad loads, Google and its partners may collect information from your browser, such as cookies, your device type and your general location, to show and measure ads. In Google's words:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can turn off personalized ads from Google in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of other companies' personalized ads at aboutads.info. To learn more, read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If your school's network or an ad blocker stops the ad from loading, the ad space simply stays hidden and the app keeps working.

Ads and younger students

Interval Lab is for anyone learning music, and students of all ages use it, including in middle school classes. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and the app itself never asks for it.

We block adult (18+) ads and other ad categories that aren't suitable for students.
